TinyAquarium — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

TinyAquarium is a cozy aquarium-raising game paired with your step count. It requires no account registration and has no third-party analytics tracking. We run no servers of our own, and we have no channel through which to read your in-game content.

HealthKit Steps

TinyAquarium accesses exactly one piece of data through Apple HealthKit, on a read-only basis: your daily walking step count (stepCount). It is used solely to calculate egg-hatching progress locally on your device. This app never writes any data to HealthKit, and it never collects, uploads, transmits, or shares your health data with any third party. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS "Settings → Privacy & Security → Health"; once revoked, egg-hatching progress stops advancing, but all other features remain unaffected.

macOS Version

The Mac app shares its codebase with the iOS app, with these differences. It does not use HealthKit and does not read any health data — macOS has no pedometer, and the Mac build links neither HealthKit nor Core Motion. Egg hatching there is driven instead by the time the app spends in the foreground on your own Mac, which is counted locally and never leaves the device. The Home Screen widget is iOS-only and is not part of the Mac app. Sending feedback on macOS opens a pre-filled message in your default mail client, addressed to us, whose body already contains the same diagnostics described under Feedback below (save-health summary and recent event log). The app itself transmits nothing; you can read or edit the draft and choose whether to send it. If you send it, we receive your email address and the message. Game saves roam between your Mac and your iPhone through the same iCloud key-value storage described above.

Local Storage

Your keeper name, tank and fish data, encyclopedia (Dex) unlock progress, Bubble Coin balance, décor layout, and all game save data are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

iCloud Sync

Game save data roams across your own Apple devices via Apple iCloud key-value storage (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore). This data is stored on Apple's infrastructure, under your own iCloud account, and the developer cannot read it. You can remove this app's data in iOS "Settings → Apple Account → iCloud".

Nearby Peer-to-Peer Fish Trading

Trading fish with nearby friends uses Apple Multipeer Connectivity, transferring data directly, end-to-end, between two devices over local network and Bluetooth — it never passes through any external server, and the developer never sees the contents of the trade. This feature requires Local Network permission and only operates while you actively have the trading screen open.

In-App Purchases

This app offers the following in-app purchase items, all processed through Apple's official App Store payment system (StoreKit 2). We never see or store your payment credentials, card numbers, or Apple account information.

  • Sunlit Starter Pouch (Consumable) — in-game Bubble Coins
  • Deep-Sea Pearl Jar (Consumable) — in-game Bubble Coins
  • Pirate's Overflowing Chest (Consumable) — in-game Bubble Coins
  • Poseidon's Golden Vault (Consumable) — in-game Bubble Coins
  • TinyAquarium Pro (Non-consumable) — a one-time unlock raising the idle earning cap, food jar capacity, breeding mutation rate, and exclusive tank themes

Bubble Coins are a purely in-game virtual currency with no cash value; they cannot be redeemed for cash or transferred, and can only be used within this app to purchase décor items and upgrades.

Rewarded Advertisements (Google AdMob)

The iOS version of TinyAquarium includes optional rewarded video advertisements provided by Google AdMob, allowing players to voluntarily watch short video clips in exchange for daily in-game bonuses (such as free Bubble Coins or egg incubation boost). In this process, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as IDFA, subject to your tracking permission), coarse location, usage data, and diagnostic data to deliver relevant ads. You can manage or revoke tracking authorization at any time in iOS "Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking". TinyAquarium Pro players enjoy all bonuses instantly without watching any ads. The macOS version contains zero advertisements.

Desktop Widget

The desktop widget displays your tank view, step count, and Bubble Coin balance. Data is shared locally on-device via an App Group container and never leaves your device.

Feedback (optional, sent through Slack)

If you send feedback from Settings → Report a Problem on iPhone and tap Send, the app sends: the message you typed; a screenshot, if you choose one from your photo library (the app does not read the rest of your photos); the app version and build number, iOS version and language/region setting; and an automatically attached diagnostics file (diagnostics.txt) containing a one-line save-health summary (sizes of the save data on the device and in iCloud, and storage flags) and the app's recent event log (up to about 32 KB of timestamps, event names, counts, byte sizes and flags, for example save, sync, purchase and reward events). The diagnostics contain no keeper name, fish names or other content you entered. It goes to a private channel in the developer's Slack workspace. Slack (Slack Technologies, LLC) delivers and stores it on our behalf as a service provider (processor), under Slack's Privacy Policy.

This only happens when you tap Send; nothing is collected or uploaded in the background. The form has no name or email field: we only get your contact details if you type them into the message. We keep reports only as long as needed to answer and fix the issue; to have one deleted, email certain.daniel+support@gmail.com. The in-game player survey is stored on your device only and is not sent to us.

Personal Information

This app requires no account registration and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers. The keeper name and fish nicknames you enter are stored only on your own device and are never sent to the developer. The only data that reaches the developer is feedback you actively send (see the section above). Ad requests on iOS (see Rewarded Advertisements) and iCloud sync (see iCloud Sync) are handled by Google and Apple, not by us.

Children

This app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please email: certain.daniel+support@gmail.com
