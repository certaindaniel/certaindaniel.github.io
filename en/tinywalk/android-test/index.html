TinyWalk for Android — Join the test

TinyWalk for Android is in closed testing on Google Play. Google needs at least 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 days in a row before the app can go public. Thank you for helping!

You need

  • An Android phone (Android 9 or later) with Google Play
  • A Google account (Gmail)

Three steps

  1. Join the Google Group TinyWalk Testers — tap Join group. You will not get any email from it.
  2. Open the test invitation and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install TinyWalk from Google Play on your Android phone.

Keep it for 14 days

Please keep the app installed and stay opted in for 14 days. Opening it once or twice is enough — no need to use it every day.

Seeing “App not available”?

Google Play is using a different Google account from the one that joined the group. Switch Play (or the browser) to the account you used in step 1, then open the invitation again. If you just joined, wait a few minutes and retry.

What is TinyWalk?

A developer tool that writes synthetic step and distance test data into Health Connect. The data stays on your phone. Remove it anytime in Settings → Delete all TinyWalk data from Health Connect. Privacy Policy

To leave the test later, open the invitation and tap Leave the program.
