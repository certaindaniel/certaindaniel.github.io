TinyWalk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

TinyWalk is a developer utility for generating synthetic HealthKit test data. It has no account system. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (see Advertising below); upgrading to Pro removes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads and offers optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for extra generation quota, served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). With Pro, no ads are shown and the ads SDK is not started. To serve ads, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as IDFA, if authorized), approximate location, usage data, and diagnostic data. This data is handled by Google under its Privacy Policy and Ads policies; the developer cannot read it. The ads SDK has no access to your HealthKit data.

The app asks whether to allow tracking via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT). If you decline, you can still use the app and watch ads. You can manage or revoke tracking authorization any time under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

HealthKit data

All HealthKit data TinyWalk generates and accesses is processed strictly on your local device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share your HealthKit data with any external servers.

Local logs

The history of datasets you generate is stored strictly in your device's local storage and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Feedback (optional)

If you choose to use Settings → Feedback to report a problem or send a suggestion, the message you type — and a screenshot if you choose to attach one — is sent to our support channel (hosted on Slack) so we can read and respond to it. This only happens when you tap Send; apart from this and the ad requests described under Advertising, TinyWalk makes no other network requests. We also include basic technical context (app version, iOS version, and your device's language setting) to help us diagnose the issue. We do not attach your name, email, or any other identifier unless you choose to type it into your message yourself.

Personal information

TinyWalk does not require registration and does not collect or store personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location, except for whatever you voluntarily include in a Feedback message.

Payments

The one-time Pro unlock is processed and secured entirely by Apple's App Store payment system (StoreKit 2). We never see or store your payment credentials.

Children

TinyWalk is a developer tool, not intended for and does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.
