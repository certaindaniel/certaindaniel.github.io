TrailPop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

TrailPop turns your trip photos into a travel video, running entirely on your device.

Photos & GPS metadata

When you select photos, TrailPop reads the GPS coordinates and timestamp already embedded in each photo's EXIF data to build your travel route on a map, and renders your travel video — all on your device. This data stays on your device and is never uploaded. TrailPop does not use your device's live location services; it only reads GPS metadata already saved inside photos you explicitly select.

Photo Library access

TrailPop also requests permission to save your exported travel video back to your camera roll. This save happens locally on your device.

Local storage

Your trip data, selected photos, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Nothing is backed up to any cloud service by TrailPop itself. If you use iCloud device backup, that's governed by Apple's iCloud terms, not by us.

Third-party services

TrailPop has no server and contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Personal information

TrailPop does not collect your name, email, contacts, browsing history, or any other personal identifier. No account or sign-up is required.

Payments

TrailPop offers optional PRO subscriptions (monthly/yearly) and a lifetime purchase, processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children

TrailPop does not knowingly collect data from children, since it does not collect personal data from any user.
