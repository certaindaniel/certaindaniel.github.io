TrailPop

Turn your trip photos into a fog-of-war travel trail video — 100% on-device.

TrailPop turns your trip photos into a turn-by-turn travel trail video. Fog-of-war unfogs as your route progresses, photos pop out in 3D at each stop, and everything renders 100% on your device — nothing uploaded.

  • Reads GPS + timestamp from your photos' EXIF automatically
  • Real turn-by-turn road navigation trace (MapKit directions)
  • Fog-of-war map unfogs as your trip progresses
  • Photos pop out in 3D at each stop
  • 6 transport modes: car, plane, scooter, hiking, train, boat
  • 3 visual themes, 15s–5min export, 9:16 for Reels/TikTok
  • 100% on-device — nothing uploaded

Does TrailPop need an internet connection?

No. Reading photos, building your route, and rendering the video all happen offline, on your device.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. TrailPop has no server — your photos, GPS/timestamp metadata, and exported videos never leave your device.

Is this a subscription?

TrailPop offers optional PRO subscriptions and a lifetime purchase via Apple's StoreKit; the free tier works without any purchase.

Does TrailPop track my location?

No. It never accesses your device's live GPS — only the GPS data already saved inside photos you choose to select.
