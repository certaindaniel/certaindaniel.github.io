TrailPop

Turn your trip photos into a map-route travel video with a map-fog reveal — rendered on your device.

TrailPop reads the GPS and time stored in your trip photos, orders your stops, traces real roads with Apple Maps and turns it all into a 9:16 route video. The map fog clears as your route progresses, photos pop out at each stop, and the video renders on your device — your photos are never uploaded.

Does TrailPop need an internet connection?

For the map, yes. Road routes, place names and map tiles come from Apple Maps, which needs a connection. Reading your photos and rendering the video happen on your device.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Your photos and exported videos never leave your device, and TrailPop has no server of its own. To draw routes and name places, the stop coordinates are sent to Apple Maps (Apple's MapKit service).

Is this a subscription?

No subscription is required. The free tier covers up to 10 waypoints at 30fps with a watermark. Optional PRO (subscription or one-time lifetime purchase via Apple) unlocks 50 photos per trip, unlimited projects, all themes and transport modes, 4K 60fps, no watermark and videos up to 5 minutes.

Does TrailPop track my location?

No. It never accesses your device's live GPS — only the GPS data already saved inside photos you choose to select.
