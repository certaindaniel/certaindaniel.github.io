TinyKeyboard — Support & FAQ

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: How do I add the keyboard?

1. Open iPhone Settings.

2. Go to General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard….

3. Under Other iPhone Keyboards, choose TinyKeyboard.

In any text field, tap the globe key to switch to it. Inside the app, Settings › Manage Keyboard walks you through the same steps.

Q2: Why does the keyboard ask for "Allow Full Access"? What works without it?

Zhuyin and English typing work without it. To turn it on, open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › TinyKeyboard and switch on Allow Full Access.

Full Access lets the keyboard read the phrases, barcodes, stickers, emoji favorites and custom roots you saved in the app (shared through an App Group on your iPhone), copy stickers and barcode images to the clipboard when you tap them, and record for voice input. The keyboard does not record your keystrokes and does not send anything over the internet. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Q3: How do I use the four-square key?

While typing, tap the four-square key at the bottom left of the keyboard, then choose Carrier, Phrases, Stickers or Emoji. Tapping a phrase, a barcode number or an emoji inserts it into the text field. Tapping a sticker, or long-pressing a barcode, copies it instead: touch and hold the text field and choose Paste.

Phrases, stickers and emoji favorites are managed in the app under Common (Phrases, Stickers, Emoji).

Q4: How do I add a carrier or membership barcode?

Open the TinyKeyboard app, go to Tools, and tap Scan Barcode to use the camera, or Add Barcode to type one in. You can save up to 30 barcodes. To show one at the register, long-press it on the keyboard to copy its image, or add the Show Carrier widget to your Home Screen to open your first barcode full screen.

Q5: How do I turn on custom root input?

In the app, open Settings › Keyboard Settings. First import a .dict.yaml root table, or add entries under My Roots (Settings › My Roots). Then switch on Custom Root Input. The keyboard's input-mode menu will then include the root mode. TinyKeyboard does not ship any root table of its own.

Q6: How does voice input work?

Turn on Full Access, tap Voice Input on the keyboard, press the microphone, speak, and press it again to finish. By default it uses Apple's on-device recognition. For the Whisper model, open Settings › Voice Input in the app and turn on Advanced Recognition: the first download is about 646 MB, and it then needs roughly 8 minutes to optimize for your iPhone, so stay in the app until it finishes.

The free version has a daily voice-dictation allowance; Pro removes it.

Q7: How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the app, go to Settings › More Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. No extra charge.
