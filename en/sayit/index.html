TinyKeyboard — Zhuyin & Custom Root Keyboard

Zhuyin typing, with a drawer of everyday tools.

A Taiwan-style Chinese keyboard with Zhuyin, English and custom root input. While you type, tap the four-square key at the bottom left to insert saved phrases, show your e-invoice carrier and membership barcodes, copy your own stickers, or add favorite emoji and kaomoji. Voice input is there when typing is awkward.

How do I add the keyboard?

Open iPhone Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard…, then choose TinyKeyboard under Other iPhone Keyboards. In any text field, tap the globe key to switch to it.

Why does the keyboard ask for 'Allow Full Access'? What works without it?

Zhuyin and English typing work without it. Full Access lets the keyboard read the phrases, barcodes, stickers, emoji favorites and custom roots you saved in the app (shared through an App Group on your iPhone), copy stickers and barcode images to the clipboard when you tap them, and record for voice input. The keyboard does not record your keystrokes or send anything over the internet. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I use the four-square key?

While typing, tap the four-square key at the bottom left. Pick Carrier, Phrases, Stickers or Emoji. Tapping a phrase, barcode number or emoji inserts it; tapping a sticker or long-pressing a barcode copies it, so touch and hold the text field and choose Paste.

How do I add a carrier or membership barcode?

In the TinyKeyboard app, open Tools, then tap Scan Barcode to use the camera, or Add Barcode to type one in. The app stores up to 30 barcodes.

How do I turn on custom root input?

In the app, open Settings › Keyboard Settings, then import a .dict.yaml root table or add entries in My Roots first. Then turn on Custom Root Input. The keyboard's input-mode menu will then include the root mode.

How does voice input work?

Turn on Full Access, tap Voice Input on the keyboard, press the microphone, speak, and press it again to finish. It uses Apple's on-device recognition by default. For the Whisper model, open Settings › Voice Input in the app and turn on Advanced Recognition; the first download is about 646 MB and it then needs roughly 8 minutes to optimize for your iPhone.
