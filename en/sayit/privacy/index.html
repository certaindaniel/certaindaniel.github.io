SayIt — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

SayIt is an iPhone dictation app and keyboard. Speech recognition runs on your device: your voice and the text it produces are not sent to us or to any cloud speech service. No account is required.

On-device speech recognition

SayIt recognizes speech with the Whisper large-v3-turbo model (via WhisperKit and Core ML) running on your iPhone. Until that model is ready, it uses Apple's speech recognition in on-device mode. Optional translation uses Apple's Translation framework on your device.

The first time you set up the voice model, the app downloads it (about 646 MB) from Hugging Face, the service that hosts the model files. That download is an ordinary web request; no audio or text is sent.

Your recordings, text and dictionary

Recent dictations (up to 200; the recognized text and, for some, the audio recording) are kept in a history on your device so you can review, replay or export them. Your custom terms, corrections and snippets are stored in the app's local storage and its App Group container, shared only with the SayIt keyboard. None of this is uploaded. Older history entries are removed automatically, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Keyboard and "Allow Full Access"

The SayIt keyboard needs Full Access only to exchange data with the SayIt app through the local App Group (to start on-device recording and receive the text). It does not record your keystrokes or send typing data over the internet.

Microphone and speech recognition permissions

The microphone is used only while you dictate in the app or through the keyboard. You can turn the microphone or speech-recognition permission off any time in iOS Settings → SayIt.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking; the identifier for vendors), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostic data, under Google's Privacy Policy and Ads policies. The ads SDK has no access to your audio or dictated text.

The app asks for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency; declining does not affect dictation. In the EEA and the UK, a consent dialog from Google's User Messaging Platform lets you grant, refuse or customize ad consent, and you can change it later in the app's Settings. SayIt Pro, a one-time in-app purchase, removes ads, and the ads SDK is then not started.

Feedback (optional, sent through Slack)

If you open Settings → Feedback and tap Send, the app sends: the message you typed; a screenshot, if you choose one from your photo library; the app version and build number, iOS version and language/region setting; and an automatically attached diagnostics file (diagnostics.txt). It goes to a private channel in the developer's Slack workspace. Slack (Slack Technologies, LLC) delivers and stores it on our behalf as a service provider (processor), under Slack's Privacy Policy.

The diagnostics file contains the voice-model status, whether the keyboard is set up, whether you have Pro, and the last 120 lines of the app's technical log. That log records processing stages, timings, counts and settings; any dictated text in it is replaced by its character count, so it contains no audio and no dictated or typed text. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and the form has no name or email field: we only get your contact details if you type them into the message. We keep reports only as long as needed to answer and fix the issue; to have one deleted, email certain.daniel+support@gmail.com.

Third parties

Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (ads and consent, free version), Hugging Face (one-time voice-model download), Slack (feedback you send) and Apple (App Store payments). We do not sell your data.

Payments

SayIt Pro is processed by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never see or store your payment details.

Children

SayIt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. AdMob is configured for a general audience. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a feedback message, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.
