TinyKeyboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

TinyKeyboard is an iPhone keyboard (Zhuyin, English and custom roots) with a companion app. Typing is handled on your iPhone, and what you save in the app stays on your device. No account is required.

Typing

Zhuyin composition, candidate words, English typing and custom root input all run inside the keyboard on your iPhone. The keyboard reads the text just before the cursor, as iOS allows every keyboard to, only to suggest the next characters. It does not record your keystrokes, and the keyboard contains no code that connects to the internet.

"Allow Full Access"

Zhuyin and English typing work without Full Access. Turning it on lets the keyboard do three things:

  • Read what you saved in the app (phrases, barcodes, stickers, emoji and kaomoji favorites, custom roots), which the app and keyboard share through an App Group on your iPhone.
  • Copy a sticker or a barcode image to the clipboard when you tap or long-press it. The keyboard only writes to the clipboard; it never reads it.
  • Record your voice for voice input.

What you save in the app

Phrases, barcodes (carrier and membership numbers), stickers, emoji favorites, custom roots and any root table you import are stored on your device, in the app's storage and its App Group container. They are not uploaded. Stickers come from the system photo picker, so TinyKeyboard sees only the images you choose and does not ask for access to your photo library. The camera is used only to scan barcodes you want to save, and scanning happens on your device.

Voice input

The microphone is used only when you use voice input. By default, recognition is done by Apple's speech recognition in on-device mode; TinyKeyboard does not use Apple's server-based recognition. If you turn on Advanced Recognition, the app downloads the Whisper large-v3-turbo model (about 646 MB) and recognizes speech on your iPhone. Either way, your voice and the recognized text are not sent to us or to any cloud speech service. Optional translation uses Apple's Translation framework on your device.

When voice input is used with the Whisper model, the keyboard briefly hands the recording to the app through the App Group; a queued recording expires after 2 minutes. Recent dictations (up to 200 items, with the recognized text and, for some, the recording) are kept in the app's History so you can review them. You can delete an item by swiping it in History. History is excluded from iCloud and device backups, older items are removed automatically, and deleting the app removes everything.

Downloads

The Whisper model is downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which hosts the model files. The download is an ordinary web request; no audio or text is sent. On newer versions of iOS, Apple may also download speech language resources through the system.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob inside the app; the keyboard contains no ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking; the identifier for vendors), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostic data, under Google's Privacy Policy and Ads policies. The ads SDK has no access to your recordings, dictated text or typing.

The app asks for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency; declining does not affect any feature. In the EEA and the UK, a consent dialog from Google's User Messaging Platform lets you grant, refuse or customize ad consent, and you can change it later in the app's Settings. TinyKeyboard Pro, a one-time in-app purchase, removes ads.

iCloud sync (Pro)

With Pro, the app can sync some personalization through your iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage): your phrases, your voice vocabulary terms and corrections, and six voice-output settings (filler removal, automatic Chinese-English spacing, punctuation style, Taiwanese mode, live captions, output language). Barcodes, stickers, emoji favorites, custom roots, History and recordings are not synced. The data is stored by Apple under your iCloud account; we cannot read it.

Feedback (optional, sent through Slack)

If you open Settings › More Settings › Feedback and tap Send, the app sends: the message you typed; a screenshot, if you choose one; the app version and build number, iOS version and language/region setting; and an automatically attached diagnostics file (diagnostics.txt). It goes to a private channel in the developer's Slack workspace. Slack (Slack Technologies, LLC) delivers and stores it on our behalf as a service provider (processor), under Slack's Privacy Policy.

The diagnostics file contains the voice-model status, whether the keyboard is set up, whether you have Pro, and the last 120 lines of the app's technical log. In release builds, that log records processing stages, timings, counts and settings; text fields in it are replaced by their character count, so it contains no recordings and no dictated or typed text. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and the form has no name or email field: we only get your contact details if you type them into the message. We keep reports only as long as needed to answer and fix the issue; to have one deleted, email certain.daniel+support@gmail.com.

Permissions

  • Microphone: voice input, only while you use it.
  • Speech recognition: Apple's on-device recognition for voice input.
  • Camera: scanning barcodes you want to save.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): personalized ads in the free version.

You can turn any of these off in iOS Settings, and the keyboard's Full Access in Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › TinyKeyboard.

Third parties

Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (ads and consent, free version), Hugging Face (one-time voice-model download), Slack (feedback you send), and Apple (App Store payments, iCloud sync, on-device speech and translation). We do not sell your data.

Payments

TinyKeyboard Pro is processed by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never see or store your payment details.

Children

TinyKeyboard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a feedback message, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.
