TinyAquarium — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

TinyAquarium is a cozy, health-connected idle aquarium companion app. No account registration, no third-party tracking, no advertising networks.

HealthKit Step & Activity Data

TinyAquarium reads your daily walking steps and active energy via Apple HealthKit solely to calculate egg incubation progress and tank energy on your local device. We do not transmit, sell, or share your HealthKit data with any external servers.

Local Storage & iCloud Sync

Your unlocked species, tank customizations, and game progress are stored locally on your device and synchronized across your Apple devices using Apple iCloud Key-Value Store. We do not operate our own database servers.

Nearby Peer-to-Peer Trade

Nearby fish trading uses Apple Multipeer Connectivity over local Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Trading happens directly between devices without passing through external servers.

Payments & Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and optional unlocks are handled securely by Apple StoreKit. We never receive or store your payment credentials.
