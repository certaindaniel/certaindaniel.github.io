Do I need to keep the app open to track steps?

No — TinyAquarium reads your steps in the background via Apple HealthKit. Walk normally and your eggs will continue to hatch.

How does Nearby Trade work?

It uses Apple Multipeer Connectivity over local Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. No accounts, no servers, and 100% private.

Does crossbreeding consume my adult fish?

No — adult parent fish stay in your tank and continue swimming happily after breeding.

Is my data stored on the cloud?

Your progress syncs seamlessly across your iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch using Apple iCloud Key-Value Store.
