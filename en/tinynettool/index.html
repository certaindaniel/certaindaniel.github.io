Does TinyNetTool need an internet connection?

Yes — its core diagnostics (ping, NAT detection, DNS lookup) work by testing your actual network connection.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. All diagnostic results are processed and shown on your device only; nothing is sent to our servers.

Is this a subscription?

No, TinyNetTool is a one-time purchase with no subscriptions or ads.

Does it see my browsing history?

No, it only performs network-level tests (ping, traceroute, DNS, port scans) — it doesn't access or track your browsing activity.
