TinyGPSEditor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

TinyGPSEditor is a photo GPS and EXIF metadata editor that runs entirely on your device.

Photos

When you choose a photo, the app reads and writes its GPS, EXIF, and date metadata on your device only. Photos are never uploaded to us or any third party.

GPS location

If you tap "current location," the app uses your device's location once to fill in coordinates. Place-name lookups use Apple's built-in geocoding service. Your location is never stored by us or sent to our servers.

Local storage

App preferences, location presets, and favorites are stored locally on your device only. There is no account and no cloud sync.

Personal information

TinyGPSEditor does not require registration and does not collect or request any personal identifier information. The app makes no network requests to the developer, and uses no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Payments

TinyGPSEditor is a one-time paid app purchased through Apple's App Store. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and we never see or store your payment information.

Children

TinyGPSEditor is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
