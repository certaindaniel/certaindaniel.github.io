  • Edit, batch-edit, or remove GPS location on photos
  • In-place editing — zero quality loss, no re-encoding
  • Edit the capture date too
  • View full EXIF metadata
  • Changes show up right in the Photos app

Does TinyGPSEditor need an internet connection?

Not for core editing — only the optional "current location" and place-name lookup use Apple's geocoding service, and no data is sent to the developer.

Is my photo or location data uploaded anywhere?

No. All GPS/EXIF reading and writing happens entirely on your device; photos are never uploaded to us or any third party.

Is this a subscription?

No, TinyGPSEditor is a one-time App Store purchase with no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No account or sign-in is required.
