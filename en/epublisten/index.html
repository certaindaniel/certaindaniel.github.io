EpubAloud: EPUB to Audiobook

Turn any EPUB into an audiobook with the natural voices built into your iPhone and Mac.

Open an EPUB and listen. The book's language is detected automatically and the most natural installed voice is used — Polish, English, Chinese, Japanese and more.

View on the App Store
Privacy Policy
  • Detects the book's language and picks the most natural installed voice
  • Enhanced and Premium voices are labelled, with steps to download them
  • Optional auto-continue to the next chapter, even with the screen locked
  • Remembers where you stopped; reading aloud works offline
  • Free to download: 15 free minutes every day (60 minutes on first launch); on iPhone, watch an ad for 15 more
  • Full Version, one-time purchase: unlimited listening and no ads, on iPhone and Mac
