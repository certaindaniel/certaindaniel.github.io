Privacy Policy
  • Lock camera focus in one tap — no more AF hunting
  • Built for digiscoping, spotting scopes & birding
  • Free to shoot photos
  • Unlock focus peaking & video recording anytime

Does FixedFocus need an internet connection?

No, all camera, focus, and recording features work fully offline.

Is my photo or video data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed on your device and saved directly to your local photo library; nothing is sent to us or any third party.

Is this a subscription?

No. The base app is free, and the Pro upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase — no subscriptions.

What does the Pro upgrade unlock?

Focus peaking, AE/EV exposure lock, HD video recording, and volume-key shutter control.
