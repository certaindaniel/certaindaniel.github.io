EpubAloud for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

EpubAloud for Android turns EPUB books into spoken audio. This policy explains how the app and its third-party services handle data. The app does not require an account, and Daniel Lu does not operate a server for the app.

Your EPUB books and reading data

The app reads a book that you select through Android's file picker. It stores the selected file reference, reading position, chapter title, voice and playback preferences, and listening-time balance in the app's private storage. EPUB files are not uploaded to a server operated by Daniel Lu. The app excludes its saved preferences and book files from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes this local data.

Language identification uses Google's bundled ML Kit language-identification model on your device. The book text and identified result are processed on the device and are not sent to Google's servers. The ML Kit SDK may send diagnostic and usage metrics to Google, such as device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, feature events and errors, and the identified language. These metrics are encrypted in transit using HTTPS and are used for diagnostics and usage analytics.

Text-to-speech

The app sends the selected text to the Android text-to-speech engine and voice that you choose. Voices marked as available offline can synthesize speech on your device. A voice that requires a network connection may send text to that voice provider's service for speech synthesis. That provider's own privacy terms apply. You can choose an offline voice in the app's voice settings to avoid network-based speech synthesis.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version uses Google AdMob for banner ads in the reader and optional rewarded video ads. A completed rewarded ad adds 15 minutes of listening time, subject to the daily limit shown in the app. The one-time Full Version purchase removes ads.

When the Google Mobile Ads SDK requests or displays ads, it automatically collects and shares with Google the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), ad-related product interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and App Set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google encrypts this data in transit using TLS. Daniel Lu does not receive these ad identifiers or the SDK's collected data. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policies.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether a consent form or a privacy-options entry point is required in your region. When required, the app presents the available choices before requesting ads. You can reopen privacy choices from Settings when that option is required for your app session. You can also manage your Android advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

The optional Full Version is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment details and provides the app with the purchase status needed to unlock the product. Daniel Lu does not receive your card or payment account details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Feedback you choose to share

If you write a report in Settings and tap Share, Android asks you to choose a destination app. Nothing is sent until you choose one. The shared report includes your message, app version, Android version, device manufacturer and model, app language, and selected voice. It does not attach the EPUB file, book text, or book title. The destination app and service you choose handle the information you share through them.

Retention and deletion

Reading data and preferences remain in the app's private storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The app has no account system or developer-operated data server. Google and the selected text-to-speech provider handle data under their own retention and deletion terms, linked above or made available by those providers.

Children

EpubAloud for Android is not designed for or directed to children under 13.
