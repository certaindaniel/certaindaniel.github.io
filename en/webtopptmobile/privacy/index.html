Web to PPT (Editable) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Web to PPT (Editable) converts HTML/React slide content into editable PowerPoint (PPTX) files and PDFs, entirely on your iPhone or iPad.

Local processing

All HTML rendering, slide processing, PDF generation, and PPTX file creation happen locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any external server.

Your content

The HTML/React code you input and the PDF/PPTX files you generate belong entirely to you. We cannot access them.

Local storage

The app stores your editing history in your device's local sandbox (UserDefaults) so you can quickly get back to recent documents. You can clear this at any time from within the app. Exported files are saved to Files or shared via the share sheet, and are not backed up to any cloud service by the app itself.

Network access

The app's built-in preview (WebView) may load external resources — such as CSS, JavaScript, or images — that your own HTML code references, but those requests are entirely controlled by your content. The app itself does not send any other network requests, and does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.

Personal information

Web to PPT (Editable) does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

Payments

Web to PPT (Editable) is a free app with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
