Web to PPT (Editable) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Web to PPT (Editable) converts HTML/React slide content into editable PowerPoint (PPTX) files and PDFs on your iPhone or iPad. No account is required. The only data the app itself sends anywhere is feedback you choose to send (see Feedback below).

Local processing

All HTML rendering, slide processing, PDF generation and PPTX creation happen locally on your device. Your documents are not uploaded to any server.

Your content

The HTML/React code you enter and the PDF/PPTX files you create belong to you. We cannot access them.

Local storage

The app keeps your editing history in its local sandbox (UserDefaults) so you can get back to recent documents; you can clear it in the app. Exported files are saved to Files or shared through the share sheet, and are not backed up to any cloud service by the app itself.

Network access

The built-in preview (WebView) may load external resources, such as CSS, JavaScript or images, that your own HTML references; those requests are controlled by your content. Apart from that and feedback you send, the app makes no network requests. It uses no third-party analytics or advertising services.

Feedback (optional, sent through Slack)

When you open Send Feedback and tap Send, the app sends the message you typed together with the app version and build number, iOS/iPadOS version, device type (iPhone or iPad), and language/region setting. Your documents are never attached. It goes to a private channel in the developer's Slack workspace. Slack (Slack Technologies, LLC) delivers and stores it on our behalf as a service provider (processor), under Slack's Privacy Policy. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and the form has no name or email field: we only get your contact details if you type them into the message. We keep reports only as long as needed to answer and fix the issue; to have one deleted, email certain.daniel+support@gmail.com.

Third parties

Slack is the only third-party service the app uses, to deliver feedback you choose to send.

Personal information

The app does not collect personally identifiable information, except anything you choose to write in a feedback message.

Payments

Web to PPT (Editable) is a one-time paid app purchased through the App Store. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases, and we never see or store your payment information.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: certain.daniel+support@gmail.com
