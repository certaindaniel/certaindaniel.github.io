Web to PPT (Editable)

Turn AI-generated HTML slides into a truly editable PowerPoint — 100% on-device.

Made slides with an AI chatbot? Turn AI-generated HTML into truly editable PowerPoint — real text & shapes, not screenshots. 100% on-device.

View on the Mac App Store
Privacy Policy
  • Paste or import AI-generated HTML slides
  • Real editable text, shapes and tables — not flattened images
  • Exports to native PowerPoint (.pptx)
  • Also exports a high-quality 16:9 slide PDF
  • 100% on-device processing — nothing uploaded
  • One-time purchase, no subscription

Does this app need an internet connection?

No — conversion, rendering, and export all happen on your Mac. An internet connection is only used if your own HTML content references external resources like images or fonts.

Is my content uploaded anywhere?

No. Your HTML/React input and the PDF/PPTX files you export stay on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Is this a subscription?

No. Web to PPT (Editable) is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store — no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Does it use analytics or ads?

No. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.
