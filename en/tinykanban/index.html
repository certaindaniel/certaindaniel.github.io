TinyKanban

Turn Google Tasks into a Kanban board on your Mac.

Drag your Google Tasks across To Do, In Progress, and Done. Move a card to Done and it is completed in Google Tasks. TinyKanban also adds menu bar access and connects directly to your Google account, with no server of its own.

Does TinyKanban store my tasks on a server?

No. TinyKanban has no server of its own. It talks directly to Google, and your tasks stay in your Google account.

Does it work offline?

No. TinyKanban loads and saves tasks through the Google Tasks API, so it needs an internet connection.

Why can't I delete a task assigned from Google Docs or Chat?

Deleting one through the Google Tasks API also deletes the original in Docs or Chat. Open the task at its source and unassign it there first.
