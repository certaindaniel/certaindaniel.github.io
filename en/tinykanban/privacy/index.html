TinyKanban — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

TinyKanban is a macOS client for Google Tasks. It has no server of its own: data travels directly between your Mac and Google. The developer cannot see your tasks or your account data.

Google user data TinyKanban accesses

When you sign in, TinyKanban asks Google for these permissions:

• Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks): your task lists and tasks, including titles, notes, due dates, completion status, and subtasks.

• Email address (userinfo.email) and basic profile (userinfo.profile): your Google account email, name, and profile picture.

TinyKanban does not access any other Google data.

How TinyKanban uses Google user data

• Google Tasks data is used only to show your tasks on the Kanban board and to make the changes you ask for: create, edit, move, complete, and delete tasks and task lists.

• Your email address and basic profile are used only to show which Google account is signed in.

TinyKanban does not use Google user data for advertising, does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models, and does not let any person read it.

Sharing, transfer, and disclosure

TinyKanban does not sell, share, transfer, or disclose Google user data to any third party. The data goes only between your Mac and Google's servers. The developer runs no server, so the developer never receives your data and has none to disclose.

Google API Services User Data Policy

TinyKanban's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How your data is protected

• You sign in on Google's own sign-in page. TinyKanban never sees your Google password.

• All requests to Google use encrypted HTTPS connections.

• Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain, which macOS encrypts and protects.

• Tasks are kept only in memory while the app runs. TinyKanban does not write your tasks to disk and has no offline task database.

• Preferences, such as window and display settings, are stored in local app settings and contain no Google user data.

• If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally through macOS.

Data retention and deletion

• Task data in memory is discarded when you quit TinyKanban.

• Sign-in tokens stay in your Keychain until you sign out. Signing out deletes them right away.

• You can revoke TinyKanban's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. After that, TinyKanban can no longer read or change your data.

• Your tasks stay in Google Tasks, where Google keeps them until you delete them. TinyKanban stores priority, reminder times, and Kanban status as text in a task's notes, because the Google Tasks API has no fields for them. Other Google Tasks apps may show this text. To remove it, edit the note or delete the task.

• Deleting the app removes its local settings. TinyKanban keeps no copy of your data anywhere else.

Third-party services

TinyKanban has no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It connects only to Google's sign-in and API services.

Children

TinyKanban is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children under 13.
