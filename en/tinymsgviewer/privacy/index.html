Tiny MSG Viewer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Tiny MSG Viewer opens Outlook .msg and standard .eml email files on your Mac or Android device. No account is required, and the app contains no advertising or analytics. Your email files are processed on your device and are not sent to us. Data leaves your device only when you start it yourself: loading remote content in an email, or sending feedback (both described below).

Your email files

All file processing happens locally. The app does not upload, transmit or share your .msg or .eml files or their contents with us or any third party, except a file you deliberately attach to Mac feedback (see below).

Remote content in emails

Emails can reference images, style sheets and fonts stored on internet servers, usually run by the sender or the sender's email service. The app blocks this remote content by default. When you choose "Load Remote Images" for an email, the app requests that content directly from those servers, which can then see:

  • that the email was opened, and when;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as your city) can be inferred;
  • the user agent of the app's web view (software and operating-system version).

These requests go from your device straight to the servers named in the email; we do not receive this data. On Android they are made over encrypted connections (HTTPS) only. If you do not choose "Load Remote Images", no such request is made.

Feedback on Mac (sent through Slack)

When you open Feedback in the Mac app and press Send, the app sends: the message you typed; one file, if you chose to attach one; and the app version and build number, macOS version, and language/region setting. It goes to a private channel in the developer's Slack workspace. Slack (Slack Technologies, LLC) delivers and stores it on our behalf as a service provider (processor), under Slack's Privacy Policy.

The attachment can be any file you pick, including an .msg or .eml email. Such an email may contain other people's names, email addresses and message content, so attach one only if you are allowed to share it with us. Nothing is sent until you press Send, and the form has no name or email field: we only get your contact details if you type them into the message. We keep reports only as long as needed to answer and fix the issue; to have one deleted, email certain.daniel+support@gmail.com.

Feedback on Android (by email)

When you choose "Send feedback", the app opens your own email app with a draft addressed to us. The draft contains the app version, the Android version and the device model, and nothing from the emails you view. Nothing is sent unless you send the draft from your email app. If you do, we receive your email address, your message, and that technical information, and use them only to answer you and fix problems.

Usage data

We do not collect usage data. The app does not track your usage or send analytics to any server.

Third parties

Slack delivers Mac feedback you choose to send. On Android, the email app and email service you choose deliver your feedback email. Servers referenced in an email receive the requests described above only if you choose "Load Remote Images". No other third-party services are used.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
