Tiny MSG Viewer

Open .msg & .eml files on your Mac — no Outlook required.

Double-click any .msg or .eml on your Mac — read sender, body and attachments instantly, with full HTML and Dark Mode. Export to PDF in one click. No Outlook, no account.

View on the Mac App Store
  • Drag & drop, or double-click in Finder to open
  • Full HTML rendering with inline images, or switch to plain text
  • Quick Look in Finder — press space to preview without opening the app
  • Export any email to PDF in one click
  • Attachments: open, preview, or save to disk
  • Parses locally on your Mac — your email never leaves your device
