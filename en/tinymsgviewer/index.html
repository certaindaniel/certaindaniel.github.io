Tiny MSG Viewer

Open .msg & .eml files on your Mac — no Outlook required.

Double-click any .msg or .eml on your Mac — read sender, body and attachments instantly, with full HTML and Dark Mode. Export to PDF in one click. No Outlook, no account.

View on the Mac App Store
  • Drag & drop, or double-click in Finder to open
  • Full HTML rendering with inline images, or switch to plain text
  • Quick Look in Finder — press space to preview without opening the app
  • Export any email to PDF in one click
  • Attachments: open, preview, or save to disk
  • Parses locally on your Mac — your email never leaves your device

Does this app need an internet connection?

No — opening and reading .msg/.eml files works fully offline. An internet connection is only used if you choose to send feedback from the app menu.

Is my email data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your .msg/.eml files are parsed entirely on your Mac. The only exception: if you use TinyMSGViewer → Send Feedback, the message you type (and a screenshot if you attach one) is sent to our support channel.

Is this a subscription?

No. Tiny MSG Viewer is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store — no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Do I need Microsoft Outlook installed?

No. Tiny MSG Viewer reads .msg and .eml files on its own — Outlook is not required.
