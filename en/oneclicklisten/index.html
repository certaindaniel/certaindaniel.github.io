OneClickListen

Turn any webpage into audio — one-tap read aloud with background play.

Turn any webpage into audio! One-tap read aloud with background play and resume. Pay once, no subscription — works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

View on the App Store Privacy Policy
  • One tap turns any webpage into audio
  • Background playback, resumes where you left off
  • Works on iPhone, iPad and Mac
  • Pay once, no subscription

Does OneClickListen send my webpage text anywhere?

No. All text-to-speech processing happens on your device using Apple's built-in voices; nothing is sent to an external server.

Does OneClickListen need an internet connection?

No — once a page is loaded, reading it aloud works fully offline; text-to-speech is done entirely on-device.

Is this a subscription?

No, it's a one-time purchase that works across iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Do I need to create an account?

No, no account or sign-in is required to use OneClickListen.
