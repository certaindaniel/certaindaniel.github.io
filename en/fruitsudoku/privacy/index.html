Fruit Sudoku Adventure — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Fruit Sudoku Adventure is an educational puzzle game designed for kids and families. We are deeply committed to protecting your and your children's privacy. The app requires no user accounts, displays no advertisements, uses no third-party analytics, and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Children's Privacy & Families Policy (COPPA / Google Play Families Policy)

Fruit Sudoku Adventure is built for children of all ages (including ages 5 and under, 6–8, and 9–12) and families. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), EU GDPR-K, and the Google Play Families Policy and Apple App Store Kids Category requirements:

  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) or device identifiers from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
  • The app contains no social features, chat functions, or external links that could expose children's personal information.

Local Storage & Game Progress

All game progression—unlocked chapters, completed levels, star ratings, best completion times, and audio settings—is stored solely on your local device (using Android SharedPreferences or iOS UserDefaults). No gameplay or progress data is uploaded to remote servers. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed by the operating system.

No Ads, No Tracking, No Third-Party SDKs

The app contains zero banner ads, interstitial ads, or rewarded ads. It embeds no third-party advertising SDKs or analytics frameworks (such as Google Analytics or Firebase). We do not track user behavior or collect telemetry.

Network Connectivity

Fruit Sudoku Adventure is completely playable offline. The core gameplay does not initiate or require any network connections.

Support & Contact

If parents or players contact us via email for support (certain.daniel+support@gmail.com), your email address and message will only be used to respond to your inquiry and resolve technical issues. We will never sell or share your contact information for marketing purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal changes. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
