TinyGtask

A native macOS menu bar app for Google Tasks.

Check and add Google Tasks from your Mac menu bar, or open the full window for lists, tags, subtasks, and notes. TinyGtask connects directly to your Google account, with no server of its own.

Does TinyGtask store my tasks on a server?

No. TinyGtask has no server of its own. It talks directly to Google, and your tasks stay in your Google account.

Does it work offline?

No. TinyGtask loads and saves tasks through the Google Tasks API, so it needs an internet connection.

Why can't I delete a task assigned from Google Docs or Chat?

Deleting one through the Google Tasks API also deletes the original in Docs or Chat. Open the task at its source and unassign it there first.
