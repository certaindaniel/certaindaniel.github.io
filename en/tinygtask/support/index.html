TinyGtask — Support & FAQ

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Does TinyGtask store my tasks on a server?

No. TinyGtask has no server of its own. It talks directly to Google, and your tasks stay in your Google account.

Q2: Does it work offline?

No. TinyGtask loads and saves tasks through the Google Tasks API, so it needs an internet connection.

Q3: Why can't I delete a task assigned from Google Docs or Chat?

Deleting one through the Google Tasks API also deletes the original in Docs or Chat. Open the task at its source and unassign it there first.

Q4: How do I sign out or remove access?

Use Sign Out in the app. To remove access entirely, revoke TinyGtask on your Google Account permissions page.

Q5: Why don't I get a reminder?

Allow TinyGtask in macOS System Settings > Notifications. A date-only task gets a 09:00 summary only if it is scheduled before 09:00 local time. Tasks with a reminder time follow that time.
