EpubAloud

Turn any EPUB into an audiobook — auto-detected language, offline.

Turn any EPUB into an audiobook. The book's language is detected automatically — Polish, English, Chinese, Japanese and more — and you can pick a voice per language.

View on the App Store Privacy Policy
  • Auto-detects the book's language, per chapter
  • Pick a different voice for each language
  • Works fully offline
  • Remembers exactly where you left off

Does this app need an internet connection?

No — reading, text-to-speech, and progress tracking all happen on your device.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your EPUB files, reading progress, and settings never leave your device.

Is this a subscription?

No. EpubAloud is a one-time purchase on the App Store — no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Since everything is stored only on your device, deleting the app removes all of it — there's nothing stored elsewhere to request deletion of.
