EpubAloud — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

EpubAloud reads your EPUB books aloud and remembers where you left off.

EPUB files

The EPUB files you select stay on your device. We do not upload or transmit them anywhere.

Local storage

Your reading position (chapter and sentence) and app preferences (voice, speech rate, pitch) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults), so the app can resume where you left off. We do not have any servers that collect or store this data.

Personal information

EpubAloud does not collect personal information, does not track your location, does not use analytics or tracking services, does not use cookies, and does not use any third-party advertising.

Payments

EpubAloud is a free app with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children

This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.
