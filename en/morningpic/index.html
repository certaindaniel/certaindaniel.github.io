Privacy Policy
  • Random beautiful photo pulled fresh each time
  • Paired with a curated inspirational quote
  • Polaroid-style card design
  • Save to Photos or share anywhere
  • Offline mode supported

Does this app need an internet connection?

Yes, to fetch new photos and serve ads. Previously cached images/quotes can still be viewed offline.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Only if you use "Contribute Quote" — that text is stored in a public Google Sheets database. AdMob also collects an advertising identifier and device info for ad delivery. Everything else (preferences, stats, cache) stays on your device.

Is this a subscription?

No. The app is free and ad-supported, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes — limit ad tracking in your device's privacy settings.
