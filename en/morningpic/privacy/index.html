Random Quote & Picture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Random Quote & Picture shows a random photo paired with an inspirational quote, styled like a Polaroid card. It is available for iPhone and Android and requires no account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. The app asks AdMob for non-personalized ads only. Even so, to serve, cap and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID, where permitted), device information (model, OS version), ad interaction data and your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred). Google handles this data under its Privacy Policy and Ads policies; we cannot read it.

On iPhone the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. You can decline and keep using the app, and change your choice any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Quotes

Quotes are downloaded from our quote list, hosted in Google Sheets and served through Google Apps Script. If you use "Contribute Quote", the quote text you submit is sent to that Google Sheets database and may be shown to other users. You can choose not to use this feature.

Images

By default the app downloads curated photos from Picsum Photos (Unsplash can be chosen instead in Settings) and caches them on your device for offline viewing. Those services see a normal web request from your device. If you use "Pick from Gallery", the selected photo is processed on your device to create your card and is never uploaded.

Local storage

Your preferences (such as the image source), favorite quotes, usage statistics, reminder settings, and cached images and quotes are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Feedback (optional, sent through Slack)

If you open Feedback and tap Send, the app sends the message you typed together with the app version and build number, your operating-system version and your language/region setting. It goes to a private channel in the developer's Slack workspace. Slack (Slack Technologies, LLC) delivers and stores it on our behalf as a service provider (processor), under Slack's Privacy Policy. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and the form has no name or email field: we only get your contact details if you type them into the message. We keep reports only as long as needed to answer and fix the issue; to have one deleted, email certain.daniel+support@gmail.com.

Third parties

Google AdMob (ads), Google Sheets / Apps Script (quote list and contributed quotes), Picsum Photos or Unsplash (photos), Slack (feedback you send), and Apple App Store or Google Play (payments). We do not sell your data.

Personal information

The app does not require an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email address, other than what you choose to write in a contributed quote or a feedback message.

Payments

The app can be used free with ads. Optional purchases (lifetime ad removal, and festival templates by subscription or one-time purchase) are processed by the Apple App Store on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: certain.daniel+support@gmail.com
